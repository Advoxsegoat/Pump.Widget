7. His slam: one dash-step straight away or a hop gets out from any distance; guarding, swaying and ducking are hit. Is that the right lesson, and is the ring on the ground plain enough?
8. The blade Knuckle: does he read as armed when he stands and walks (the sword is 0.75 m and draws as a short bright blade at this size: should it be longer? his reach would grow with it)?
9. Dodge his cut (duck) or his chop (sway) and counter: does it land every time, and is the backcut that follows the cut fair?
10. His cut reaches 2.4 m: a player walking back from 2.2 m is still hit. Too long?
11. His lunge now comes in every fight, from close (1.7 to 2.4 m): is 40 frames of warning enough to catch it or dash away?
12. When he is hit he keeps his sword in view, but the five hit poses differ little from one another: do they read as a man being hit?
13. Beside another enemy the heavy now waits for a free turn and nobody else attacks while he does: he starts about 40% fewer attacks beside a tier I (his longest wait is about 5 s). Does the yard's last wave still feel like its hardest, and does it feel right that the others hold back for him?

## Owner checklist for the menus

1. Pause in the middle of a blow, a slow-down and the Drummer's string (Esc or Start), wait, resume: does the fight come back unchanged?
2. Hold guard, pause, resume still holding: the guard should be up. Charge a heavy, pause, resume, let go: one heavy with the whole charge.
3. Close the menu with the dash-step button, the palm button and Esc in turn: he must not dash, strike or reopen the menu.
4. Try the menu with the pad's stick (a flick moves one row; held, it repeats) and with the keys; a sloppy diagonal on the stick moves by whichever way it crossed first.
5. Resize the window while paused; it will not go below 640×360.
6. Does the panel sit well at the top left, or does it cover something you want to see while paused?
7. Settings: turn the afterimage and the slow-down off and fight a Flurry; set shake to 0 and take a hit; set Master to 0 and back; hold Left on a sound slider and listen to how often the sample plays.
8. Window: switch to a window at ×2 and ×3 and back to fullscreen on your real screen, and press F11 with the Size row selected (none of this could be tried on a real screen here).
9. Quit and start again: is everything as you left it? Delete `settings.cfg` by hand and start: are the defaults back without a word?
10. On and Off are both drawn in gold and are told apart by the word only: should Off be dim?
11. Controls: rebind Palm on the keyboard and on the pad and fight with it; bind a key another action already has and read the line under the page; press Esc in the middle of a capture; press F5 or F2 in the middle of a capture and read the refusal; unplug the pad while the controls page is open; restore the defaults.
12. Do the bindings line up in a column you can read at a glance, and is a long key name (cut with …) still clear?

## Owner checklist for recording and replay

1. Play a fight started with `-- --record=out/replays/mine.tres`: pause in the middle, switch the slow-down off in Settings, resume, fight on, quit. Start again with `-- --replay=out/replays/mine.tres` and watch: is it your fight, move for move, and does it end with `as recorded`?
2. Do the same on the mouse and on the pad. A recording made on one replays the same whichever is plugged in.
3. After a replay, are your own settings (sound, shake, the window) as you left them?

## Owner checklist for the Director

1. Look at the five framings with a line showing (`tools/test.sh --render --filter=test_cine_shots` writes `out/story/cine_*.png` at 640×360 and ×3): wide, medium, close, insert, over-the-shoulder, and the close shot from four sides. Does a close shot show enough of the person (the camera drops from the fight's −40° to −25°), and is the dialogue box in the lower bar the right place for it?

3. The words are the HUD's soft anti-aliased font, typed at 30 characters a second with a blip on every second character (about 8 dB under a palm's impact): the right speed and sound?

## Filing a replay

Start the game with `-- --record=<path>`, play, and quit: the file is written as the game ends. Attach the `.tres`.
Anyone replays it with `-- --replay=<path>` on the same game version; a fight that differs says at which frame.

## Known limits / notes for later

- **The engine dies now and then in long bot runs** (signal 11, `propagate_notification` on `/root` from a thread: about 3 in 60 runs of one process, more on a loaded machine). `run_bot.gd` plays each seed in a Godot of its own and runs it again if it dies; worked round, not fixed.
- **A bot fight does not read the cursor or the window**: the proof is the spy test (nothing reads the cursor while the bot plays). The window-and-mouse test beside it is a guard that a headless run cannot make fail today.
- **A recording replays on the same game version only.** Another format is refused by its number; a fight that differs says at which frame.
- **Forward+ parity.** Forward+ is the reference look, but the container has no GPU. CI now checks the Forward+ branches on every push, not only on a local run: the `render-forward-plus` job installs lavapipe (a software Vulkan driver) and runs `test_screen_post` (which includes the Forward+ against Compatibility check), `test_toon_palette` and `test_toon_world` in the real Forward+ renderer, and it is green. The whole suite also passes under `tools/test.sh --forward-plus` with lavapipe here. So the branches work and agree with Compatibility. That says nothing about speed or about how it looks on a real GPU, so your F4 comparison on the PC is still the last check.
- **Compatibility is washed out.** The container and a web build use Compatibility, which draws the world pastel and washed out: the engine adds shadowed light passes in sRGB. Forward+ on the PC is the reference look. A `toon_world` Compatibility branch is a follow-up before any web build. The web build is made by CI but has never been opened in a browser (see the owner checklist for how to serve it). It starts in its page, not fullscreen, because a browser refuses fullscreen until the player has pressed a key or clicked (`display/window/size/mode.web` in `project.godot`); the browser's own fullscreen works. Whether the game's Alt+Enter works there is untried.
- **No strafe clips.** Orientation warping covers 8-way movement. `tools/anim/retarget.py` is ready for CMU clips; check the legs on the first one (the CMU rest splay).
- **Liveliness uses engine internals.** It is applied once per tick through Godot 4.7.2 internals: the skeleton's manual callback plus `NOTIFICATION_UPDATE_SKELETON`. Re-check it on any engine upgrade.
- **`courtyard.tscn` and `training_yard.tscn` are the source** after generation. Do not run `build_courtyard.gd` or `build_region.gd` on them again; edit the scenes.
- **Nodes under an instance need `[editable path="..."]`.** A scene that hangs nodes under a child of an instanced scene (as `main.tscn` does under `PixelStage/World`) must list that instance as editable, or the export drops those nodes and the build fails at startup. In the editor this is "Editable Children". The test `test_every_scene_keeps_all_its_nodes_through_the_exports_repack` catches it.
- **Exports.** Godot does not create the output folder: `mkdir -p out/linux out/windows out/web` first. The builds leave out `tests/` and `tools/`: nothing the game loads may live there (the game starts with `TestLogger` from `core/test_logger.gd`; `tests/tools/test_export_presets.gd` checks it). A zip from CI loses the Linux build's executable bit: `chmod +x`. No icon and no code signing yet.

## How to run

From the repository root:

```bash
bash all-divine/tools/setup.sh                 # once: Godot into all-divine/.tools/godot/ (add --templates, --bpy)
all-divine/tools/test.sh                       # every headless test; --filter=<part of a test id>
all-divine/tools/test.sh --render              # also the tests that need a display (xvfb, gl_compatibility)
all-divine/tools/test.sh --render --shard=2/3  # every third test file from the second: CI's render job runs shards 1/3, 2/3 and 3/3 side by side
all-divine/tools/test.sh --render --update-golden --filter=test_screen_post
                                               # rewrite tests/render/golden/post_scene_compat.png, the screen look in
                                               # Compatibility, which a --forward-plus run compares Forward+ against
all-divine/tools/godot.sh --render <args...>   # any Godot call with a display; without --render it is --headless
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/bot/run_bot.gd -- --seeds=1-10 --frames=7200 --policy=mixed --reaction=14
                                               # the combat bot: a line for each seed (waves, deaths, problems); --policy=all, --detail
all-divine/tools/godot.sh --render -- --record=out/replays/session.tres   # play; the recording is written as the game ends
all-divine/tools/godot.sh --render -- --replay=out/replays/session.tres   # the same fight again: `as recorded`, or the frame it diverged at
python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py fetch all   # download, hash-check and extract the asset packs into .cache/ (--accept-new records a changed hash)
python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/import_kenney.py     # copy the listed Kenney town pieces into assets/env/kenney_town/
python3 -I -m unittest discover -s all-divine/tools/assets/tests   # the Python tools' tests
python3 -I -m unittest discover -s all-divine/tools/audio/tests    # the sound tool's tests (and that assets/audio/sfx/ is what it makes)
python3 -I all-divine/tools/audio/make_sfx.py            # write assets/audio/sfx/*.wav, our sound effects (seeded: the same bytes every run); then tools/test.sh reimports them
all-divine/tools/bpy_test.sh                   # the bpy tools' tests (tools/anim/tests; builds Abarax and retargets synthetic BVHs into temp files)
all-divine/tools/.venv/bin/python -I all-divine/tools/anim/build_abarax.py \
    --ubc "$(python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py path ubc)" --outfits "$(python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py path outfits)" \
    --out all-divine/assets/characters/abarax/abarax.glb   # rebuild Abarax's model (same bytes every time), then tools/test.sh reimports it
all-divine/tools/.venv/bin/python -I all-divine/tools/anim/retarget.py --bvh clip.bvh --map all-divine/tools/anim/bonemap_cmu.json \
    --target all-divine/assets/characters/abarax/abarax.glb --tpose-clip A_TPose --name clip --out all-divine/out/retarget/clip.glb \
    --tpose "$(python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py path ual1)/Universal Animation Library[Standard]/Unreal-Godot/UAL1_Standard.glb"
                                               # a CMU BVH (cgspeed joint names) onto Abarax's skeleton, by world-space rotation change from rest on top
                                               # of the T-pose reference; the hips travel is scaled by hip height. Writes Abarax's model with the one clip
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/anim/build_library.gd -- --ual1 "$(python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py path ual1)" \
    --ual2 "$(python3 -I all-divine/tools/assets/fetch.py path ual2)"   # fetch.py fetch ual2 first
                                               # rebuild assets/anim/abarax/ from assets/anim/clips.cfg (marks on existing clips are kept), then godot.sh --import
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/clip_check.gd   # every move's marks and every actor's required clips against the rules; prints "clip_check: N moves, M actors OK" or each problem, exit 1
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/story_check.gd  # every story scene's shots, walks, files and marks against the rules (--final also refuses placeholders; --scene=<id> for one); prints "story_check: N scenes OK" or each problem, exit 1
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/anim/set_marks.gd -- res://assets/anim/abarax/palm_jab.tres marks.json
                                               # write a clip's marks from [{"frame": 10, "kind": "hitbox_on", "args": {"id": "palm_l"}}, ...]; replaces its Marks tracks
all-divine/tools/godot.sh --render -s res://tools/sheets/contact_sheet.gd -- --def res://data/actors/abarax.tres --out out/sheets/abarax.png
                                               # a contact sheet of the actor's clips in its palette (--clips a,b for some; --rig <rig.tscn>
                                               # instead of --def for a bare rig in its imported materials); a relative --out lands under all-divine/
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/art/make_textures.gd   # write assets/env/textures/flagstone.png (seeded: the same bytes every run)
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/world/build_region.gd -- res://world/training_yard/layout.txt res://world/training_yard/training_yard.tscn
                                               # how a region is made from its layout (the courtyard's wrapper is build_courtyard.gd), once:
                                               # the scene is the source now, and building again would overwrite whatever was changed in it
all-divine/tools/godot.sh -- --selftest        # the game (main/main.tscn) fights one tier I Knuckle in the training yard through a scripted
                                               # circuit for 600 physics frames and exits 0, or 1 if anything logged an error from startup
                                               # on, or if the circuit never began (core/self_test.gd); add --fixed-fps 60 for full speed
bash all-divine/tools/setup.sh --templates     # once, for exports: the export templates (1.3 GB download, cached in .cache/)
mkdir -p all-divine/out/linux all-divine/out/windows all-divine/out/web
all-divine/tools/godot.sh --export-release "Linux" out/linux/all-divine.x86_64   # likewise "Windows Desktop" out/windows/all-divine.exe
                                               # and "Web" out/web/index.html (presets in export_presets.cfg; paths are relative to all-divine/)
all-divine/out/linux/all-divine.x86_64 --headless -- --selftest   # the built game's own self-test: exit 0
(cd all-divine/out/web && python3 -m http.server 8000)  # then open http://localhost:8000; the .wasm does not load from file://
all-divine/tools/test.sh --forward-plus --filter=test_screen_post # the real Forward+ renderer; needs Vulkan (a GPU, or lavapipe: apt install mesa-vulkan-drivers)
all-divine/.tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_linux.x86_64 --path all-divine
                                               # play (starts fullscreen; F11 or Alt+Enter for a 1280×720 window and back):
                                               # WASD or the left stick to move, Space/Shift (A) to dash-step (hop if still) and hold to
                                               # sprint, Q (LB) to guard, with a direction pressed while it is held: sway (A, D, W) or duck (S), left mouse (RB) to jab and again for the cross and the hook, right
                                               # mouse (RT) for the heavy palm, held to charge; F1 the controls list, F3 the debug overlay, F4 the
                                               # screen look off and on, F5 the yard or the courtyard, F2 the Bilibara stretch (a blockout), F6 to F8 a tier I, II or III Knuckle,
                                               # F9 resets the fight, Esc (Start) to pause
```

Every file under `assets/` needs a row in [`ASSETS.md`](ASSETS.md); `tests/tools/test_ledger.gd` checks it.

A test is a method named `test_*` in a `tests/**/test_*.gd` file that extends `TestCase`. Its id is `res://tests/area/test_x.gd::test_y`.
