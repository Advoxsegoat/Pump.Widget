### The sway-and-duck defence

- Sway-defence Task 1: the rules of an evade. A move can carry an `evade` (SWAY or DUCK) and an `evade_on`/`evade_off` window (frame 2 on); while it is open `Combat.resolve` avoids every ordinary blow and grab as `EVADED` (a sweep or thrust still lands), perfect when the dodge matches the blow's new `line` (STRAIGHT: sway, ROUND: duck, a grab: either). An evaded blow is spent: no freeze, no push, no pair, not forgotten by the scanner, `runner.evaded` set. The hooks and the heavy are ROUND. The clip checker holds one window opening on frame 2.
- Sway-defence Task 2: the sixteen sway, duck and dash-step moves and clips (the spec's three tempos, planted feet, dash-steps 1.3 m eased over ten frames), `MoveData.directional` and `redodge_frame`; nothing starts them yet. The clip checker seeds the base sways, ducks and dash-step (`NOT_YET_STARTED`, to go when Task 3 names them in the moveset and the windows). The builder gained an `@pelvis_move` layer entry and `root_travel`; the checker lets a sway, duck or dash-step have no iframes, and follows `directional`.
- Sway-defence Task 3: sway and duck from the pad and the keys, the planted guard, the dash-step in the roll's place. `Inputs.direction_pressed_this_frame` (a move key that went down, or the left stick flicked past 0.6 and let go below 0.35) and `PlayerBrain` turn guard held (or going down that frame) plus a direction into a `sway`, or a `duck` within 45 degrees of straight down, with its world `evade_dir` (a guard press of the same frame is dropped; a direction held before guard starts nothing). `Actor.move_for` and `MoveData.side_of` pick the side from the direction against his facing, at both places the runner starts a press; `CombatTuning.guard_planted` (true for Abarax) gives the guard stance no legs, and a sway or duck that ends with guard held brings the stance back. The dash-step replaced the roll (its move, clip, cues and `clips.cfg` entry deleted; a dash-step keeps his facing and is never invulnerable, so `MoveData.invulnerable` went, and the checker's rule is that a DODGE with no evade carries no evade or iframes marks): every window that took the roll takes `dodge: dash_step`, `sway: sway_left_slip` and `duck: duck_slip`, and the guard raise, parry, catch and guard_hit take sway and duck too (their frames are in the fix round below). Controls list, self-test circuit (approach, jab, cross, hook, sway, duck, dash-step, guard, heavy) and the preview reel (the thrust is caught, the Knuckle is dashed in on) follow. `ClipChecker.NOT_YET_STARTED` is down to the four Quick and Flurry heads, for Task 4's chain to name. Fix round 1: a sway or duck opens earlier than the rest of each defence's window so none is late or lost (guard raise f2, parry f3, guard hit f6, catch f8; palm, heavy and dodge stay where they were), and a parry or catch played out with guard held ends in the guard stance like the raise, the guard hit and the sways. The dash-step travels along the stick's own direction (the side only picks the clip; its four sides are equal quarters, 45 and 135 degrees, held on the move as `side_ahead_deg` and `side_back_deg`; a sway's stay 30 and 150) and still keeps his facing. A pad with no lock and guard held turns him to the nearest foe within `aim_assist_range`. `Inputs` keeps the direction latch fresh every physics frame, paused or not, so a direction that went down while he could not think (a grab, a finisher, death, a pause) is not a press. Not changed: an attack's recovery, a flinch and a stagger still hold him (the price of the attack or of the blow). Timing as the player meets it: protection is read at the start of a frame, so a sway pressed 3 or more frames before a blow lands evades it (measured against a tier I Knuckle's jab: the Slip sway evades when pressed 3 to 12 frames before it lands). `Inputs` now runs while the tree is paused, so the last device and the mouse-sweep latch also keep up through a pause.
- Sway-defence Task 4: the chain. `DodgeChain` (numbers in `data/tuning/chain_open_hand.tres`, on Abarax) counts defences that succeed in a row: each evade, parry or catch he takes links it, a perfect dodge links two and gives its cost and four more back (once a dodge, however many blows it slips; the chain links per blow), and the count (Slip to 2, Quick 3 to 6, Flurry from 7) picks the move of every sway and duck (`Actor.move_for`: 22/16/12-frame sways, 26/18/14-frame ducks, 6/4/3 and 8/5/4 stamina). From Quick on each evade takes 4 (8 when perfect, 8 and 16 at Flurry) posture from its attacker, with no reaction unless it breaks, so a string dodged through can break it; a blow that lands at Quick or Flurry staggers him whatever its weight. An attack or a finisher cashes the chain (`Actor.cashed_tempo` for Task 5), a dash-step, hop, walk, 90 live frames or a death end it, a landed blow, a grab or a broken guard knocks him out, a guarded blow does nothing, and the frame's outcome is decided once (an evaded jab and a landed sweep together: knocked out, no link). After a sway or duck that evaded nothing, no sway, duck or dash-step starts before its redodge frame, counted on his own clock from its start whatever plays between, so mashing covers at most half of any stretch. The clip checker reads the chain as a source of started moves (`NOT_YET_STARTED` is gone) and holds its lists: three entries each, a sway list of SWAY moves and a duck list of DUCK moves, each with an evade window. Playing it: parry or dodge a blow to start the chain, keep dodging in place and each dodge gets shorter and tighter while the attacker's balance goes; attack to cash it in, stand and be hit and you are staggered.
- Sway-defence Task 5: counters and clear-outs. A counter or clear-out cashes the chain: damage and posture x1, x1.5, x2 by the tempo reached, clear-outs throw 2, 2.5, 3 m (20 stamina, stopped by walls and bodies; the paired and the dead are not thrown, a broken foe is and stays finishable), and a Flurry counter staggers whatever the poise; a counter refused for stamina cashes nothing. The back palm parts those in front of him to their own sides (a foe on his left goes left, on his right or dead ahead goes right), the four moves start without dragging a planted foot (each begins as the guard stance and eases into its pose, and a move with `plant_feet` holds the feet through its blend) and recover to the guard stance instead of holding a pose; `tools/anim/key_counters.gd` makes their layers. `MoveData.counters` and `cashes`, `HitProfile.arc_deg`, `throw` and `throw_way`, `HitEvent.power`, `always_staggers` and `throw`, hitboxes `clear_arc` and `clear_ring` on Abarax's rig, and the clip checker holds `counters` (`tools/test.sh`: 1104 passed, 0 failed; with `--render`: 1168 passed, 0 failed; `tools/clip_check.gd`: 49 moves, 4 actors OK; `--selftest` exit 0).
- F1: the sway-and-duck defence: sways, ducks, the chain, counters and clear-outs, the dash-step. Sway-defence Task 6, what the chain looks and sounds like, the slow-down, the HUD: `ChainFx` (a child of every actor whose def has a chain; `fx/chain_fx.gd`) listens to the chain and to `got_hit` and decides once a frame what is heard and shown: the cue `evade_whoosh` at pitch 2^(min(count, 12) / 12) (a semitone a link, an octave at the twelfth; the cue has no jitter), an `Afterimage` and the cue `perfect_dodge` on a perfect dodge and an afterimage on every dodge at Flurry, a white-gold rim on Abarax from Quick on (at Quick it breathes between 0.35 and full strength on a 24-frame cycle of his own clock, always the same white-gold, so it is a glow and not a dark outline; steady and full at Flurry, through `Rig.hold_aura`, the stencil-masked hull a tell's rim is, which gives way to a tell and to his parry's burst and takes the body back after them), the hard cue `knocked_out` (only for a knock-out at Quick or Flurry; a second call starts over. `Afterimage` is the body's own meshes on a skeleton of its own held in the pose he held when the dodge began (`Afterimage.capture`, the stance he left), aging on his own clock (a frame the slow-down or a hit-stop holds him on does not age it) over 12 frames, so it hangs through the slow-down (a render test counts its pixels outside every body on real dodges); ## Owner checklist for the sway-and-duck defence

1. The three tempos' feel: sway and duck from a cold start at Slip, then keep succeeding until the chain is at Quick and at Flurry. Does each tempo feel like the next, quicker and tighter, and does the rim and the rising whoosh tell you which you are in without a number?
2. The screen-relative sways when he faces sideways (the sway goes to the side of the screen the key points at, whichever way he faces).
3. The slow-down on the first perfect dodge and on entering Flurry: is it a welcome beat or does it make the fight sticky? (`slowdown_on`, `slow_frames` and `slow_share` in `data/tuning/readability.tres`.)
4. Being knocked out of a chain: is the hard cue, the rim going out and the stagger a plain enough loss? Should the knock-out also throw him back out of a string's reach (today one slip in a fast string can cost the rest of it)?
5. The dash-step's reach (1.3 m over 10 frames, never invulnerable) against the hop and the old roll's habit.
6. Mashing sways against the easiest enemy: it loses to timing but is not worthless. Is that right?
7. The pad's guard turning him to the nearest foe with no lock-on.
8. The catch being the slowest defence to sway out of.
9. The counters' arm-raised follow-through and the wave's palm to the ground.
10. The afterimage on the lamplit ground (a pale lilac copy) and on the dark: can you see it in play at the window's scale?

## Owner checklist for the new Knuckles

1. The Drummer's string: is one slip too dear (a slip costs up to 40 HP and a stagger), is the 24-frame warning enough, and are its red rim and low whoosh unmistakable? (The rim is dim for the first six frames.)

## Known limits / notes for later

- **Forward+ parity.** Forward+ is the reference look, but the container has no GPU. CI now checks the Forward+ branches on every push, not only on a local run: the `render-forward-plus` job installs lavapipe (a software Vulkan driver) and runs `test_screen_post` (which includes the Forward+ against Compatibility check), `test_toon_palette` and `test_toon_world` in the real Forward+ renderer, and it is green. The whole suite also passes under `tools/test.sh --forward-plus` with lavapipe here. So the branches work and agree with Compatibility. That says nothing about speed or about how it looks on a real GPU, so your F4 comparison on the PC is still the last check.
- **Compatibility is washed out.** The container and a web build use Compatibility, which draws the world pastel and washed out: the engine adds shadowed light passes in sRGB. Forward+ on the PC is the reference look. A `toon_world` Compatibility branch is a follow-up before any web build. The web build is made by CI but has never been opened in a browser (see the owner checklist for how to serve it). It starts in its page, not fullscreen, because a browser refuses fullscreen until the player has pressed a key or clicked (`display/window/size/mode.web` in `project.godot`); the browser's own fullscreen works. Whether the game's Alt+Enter works there is untried.
- **No strafe clips.** Orientation warping covers 8-way movement. `tools/anim/retarget.py` is ready for CMU clips; check the legs on the first one (the CMU rest splay).
- **Liveliness uses engine internals.** It is applied once per tick through Godot 4.7.2 internals: the skeleton's manual callback plus `NOTIFICATION_UPDATE_SKELETON`. Re-check it on any engine upgrade.
- **`courtyard.tscn` and `training_yard.tscn` are the source** after generation. Do not run `build_courtyard.gd` or `build_region.gd` on them again; edit the scenes.
- **Nodes under an instance need `[editable path="..."]`.** A scene that hangs nodes under a child of an instanced scene (as `main.tscn` does under `PixelStage/World`) must list that instance as editable, or the export drops those nodes and the build fails at startup. In the editor this is "Editable Children". The test `test_every_scene_keeps_all_its_nodes_through_the_exports_repack` catches it.
- **Exports.** Godot does not create the output folder: `mkdir -p out/linux out/windows out/web` first. The builds leave out `tests/` and `tools/`: nothing the game loads may live there (the game starts with `TestLogger` from `core/test_logger.gd`; `tests/tools/test_export_presets.gd` checks it). A zip from CI loses the Linux build's executable bit: `chmod +x`. No icon and no code signing yet.
