# The courtyard, as tools/world/build_courtyard.gd reads it:
#   tools/godot.sh -s res://tools/world/build_courtyard.gd
# writes world/courtyard/courtyard.tscn. That scene is the source from then on: change the scene in the editor,
# not this file (building again would overwrite the scene and lose the changes).
#
# Metres: x runs east, z south (toward the camera, which looks north), y up. Yaw is in degrees about +Y; a Kenney
# piece at yaw 0 is as modelled. Kenney pieces are 2.5 times their size (a 2.5 m tile, a wall 2.5 m tall). A
# piece, lantern or marker stands on the ground under it (the floor, or the terrace's top) unless y= says.
#
#            north (-z): a terrace 1.25 m high along the north wall, with banners on the wall behind it
#    x=-15 +---------------------------------------------------------------+ x=15   z=-12.5
#          | T hh            L     |b|   |b|   |b|   |b|     L          hh T |
#          | hh  ^StairsTop                                    RampTop/  hh  |
#          |=====[  stairs (6 steps)  ]=================[  ramp 18.4 deg  ]=|  z=-7.5 (the terrace's front)
#          |     [                    ]                 [                 ]  |
#          |                        L  bench  L          [                 ]  |  z=-3.75
#          |                         ( fountain )                             |  z=0
#          |                        L          L                             |
#          | hh                         @ Spawn                     stall hh |
#          | T hh                                       cart          hh  T |
#          +--------------------------- half wall ---------------------------+  z=12.5
#
# Commands, one a line. A # at the start of a line, or followed by a space, starts a comment (a colour's # does not).
#   floor W D                       the floor: a toon_world box W x D with its top at y = 0, in the flagstones
#                                   (larger than the courtyard, so the ground goes on past the walls)
#   terrace X0 Z0 X1 Z1 H           a raised floor H high over that rectangle, its top in the flagstones
#   stairs X0 Z0 X1 Z1 N UP         N steps over that rectangle, climbing toward UP (n, e, s or w) to the ground
#                                   past it: steps in the flagstones on ik_surface, a walk ramp through the middle
#                                   of every tread on world
#   ramp X0 Z0 X1 Z1 UP             a wedge over that rectangle from the floor up to the ground past its UP end
#   edge PIECE X0 Z0 X1 Z1 FACING [solid]
#                                   PIECE (a wall-like Kenney piece: a slab along one side of its tile) every 2.5 m
#                                   along the line, its face on the line and toward FACING, the slab behind it;
#                                   solid: a collision box behind the face, at least 1 m deep
#   piece PIECE X Z [YAW] [y=Y]     a Kenney piece; it collides as models/<PIECE> says in build_courtyard.gd
#   lantern X Z [shadow]            a lantern with its lamp (see lamps); shadow: the lamp casts shadows
#   lamps COLOUR RANGE ENERGY UP    every lantern's lamp: an OmniLight3D UP metres above the lantern's foot
#   marker NAME X Z [YAW]           a Marker3D (the way it faces is its +Z, as an actor's)
#   poi NAME X Z UP                 a Marker3D UP metres above the ground, in the group poi: a thing to glance at
#   sun PITCH YAW COLOUR ENERGY     the DirectionalLight3D, with shadows
#   sky COLOUR                      the background
#   ambient COLOUR ENERGY           the ambient light
#   grade PATH                      the region's GradeProfile

# The walls' inner faces enclose 30 x 25 m, centred on the origin.
floor 52 46
sky #2e2236
ambient #d8a3ad 0.3
