# Dark Satire TikTok: Series Bible

The world is ending, and the feed keeps going.

30–45 second stylized AI-animated shorts. Society falls apart from corporate rot, environmental collapse and everyday danger, and everyone (victims, bystanders, perpetrators) keeps scrolling.

## The Formula (every episode)

| Act | Beat | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Subtle/Absurd Threat** | A real hazard is happening on screen in the first second. |
| 2 | **The Digital Distraction** | A character finds a viral video that parodies that exact threat. |
| 3 | **The Absurd Escalation** | The consequence lands. Nobody looks up. Scrolling continues into the final frame. |

## Global Style Lock (prepend to every AI prompt)

> Vertical 9:16, stylized 3D animated characters with slightly exaggerated proportions, dystopian satire, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, desaturated concrete grays and sickly olive shadows, the ONLY saturated light sources are glowing smartphone screens (cold cyan-white) and toxic neon accents, volumetric haze, cinematic 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain.

## Narrator

One consistent voice across every episode: **calm, deadpan, nature-documentary narrator**. Never raises its voice. Pauses for a beat before each punchline. VO budget: about 110–135 words per 40–45 seconds.

## Recurring Universe (build the lore, then monetize it)

- **BreezeCorp™ / OmniCorp™**: the megacorp behind every disaster. Its logo should show up in every episode (AC units, park signs, push notifications).
- **SKRONGLE 🥐**: the in-universe brainrot meme. A crude croissant with googly eyes and human teeth that screams "SKRONGLE!!" Reuse it across episodes until viewers start commenting it and using the sound themselves. That's the merch and IP play: plushies, stickers, a "Skrongle" sound for duets.
- **The Push Notification Punchline**: every episode ends with a corporate notification or a stat ticking up while the human is down.

## Production Notes

1. **Hook in 1 second:** the threat and the phone glow are both in frame one, with POV-style text overlay.
2. **Seamless loop:** the final frame's audio matches the first frame's, so rewatches stack.
3. **Character consistency:** make a character reference sheet first, then run image-to-video with the Style Lock.
4. **Stay readable as satire:** stylized 3D, cartoonish villains, no photoreal minors, no on-screen violence. It hits harder and keeps the account inside platform guidelines.
